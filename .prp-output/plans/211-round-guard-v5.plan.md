## Invariant (replaces v4's)

**Invariant:** R is the number of distinct run ids (`[0-9]{8}T[0-9]{6}Z-[0-9a-f]{8}`, lower-cased) in the input. Every footer the runner posts is byte-exact (the adapter checks `Generated-by: prp-review-agents v<ver> run=<id> pass=<name>` before posting). The guard refuses (exit 2) when:
- a `run=` marker is not followed by a readable id, or
- a line has `generated…by` (0-20 non-alphanumerics between the words) **and** `prp-review-agents` but no id.

A footer's round can therefore go uncounted without a refusal only if the comment is edited to remove all three markers. Edited comments are out of scope (#213).

**Verified by:**
- the R5 rows below: the 2-space and tab gaps refuse, Jenkins/graphviz/Sphinx lines count 0 with exit 0;
- mutants M4a and M4b, each killed by a named row;
- the real sample: `325 of 999`, exit 0, under gawk and mawk.
